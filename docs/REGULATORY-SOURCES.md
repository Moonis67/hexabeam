## Copy safeguards

- No eligibility approval, bank account, visa, licence, tax result, processing time or professional outcome is guaranteed.
- No universal mainland/free-zone/offshore recommendation is made.
- Legal representation, audit opinions, certified translation and formal valuation reports are attributed to appropriately qualified providers.
- Exact rates, thresholds, lists of documents and processing times are not hard-coded into marketing pages; case review and official-source verification are required.
