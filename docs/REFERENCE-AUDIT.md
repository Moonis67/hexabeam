# Reference audit

Checked 10 August 2026: supplied Shuraa homepage, trade-licence and bank-account screenshots; current Shuraa website; Make Fortune; and Freemont Oneworld services.

## 1. Information hierarchy

Shuraa makes breadth immediately visible, then repeatedly narrows the user into formation, jurisdiction, supporting services, process, evidence, FAQs and conversion. Make Fortune and Freemont use fewer claims and calmer institutional grouping. Hexa Beam should combine visible breadth with a more restrained editorial hierarchy.

## 2. Navigation strategy

Deep grouped navigation works because users often know their outcome but not the exact service name. Hexa Beam adopts six plain-language service families and text-led mega panels. It avoids a large ecommerce-style icon wall.

## 3. Header behaviour

The effective pattern is a slim utility layer, sticky white primary header, prominent consultation action and accessible mobile menu. The utility layer must contain only confirmed contact data.

## 4. Footer architecture

A deep footer is a second service directory. Hexa Beam exposes priority services, company/legal links and contact status without unsupported accreditation or partner logos.

## 5. Homepage structure

The reference logic is substantial: proposition, service scanning, formation depth, business lifecycle, proof, process, FAQ and repeated consultation. Hexa Beam uses this density while replacing promotions, pricing, numerical claims and testimonials with useful service-system explanations.

## 6. Service-page structure

The supplied screenshots validate: breadcrumb → focused hero → main explanation + consultation sidebar → audience/scope → process → documents → considerations → FAQ → final CTA. Hexa Beam implements this structure without copying exact geometry.

## 7. Lead-generation mechanisms

Persistent header CTA, contextual hero CTA, sticky desktop consultation form, WhatsApp action, FAQ reassurance and final CTA. Static deployment means the form requires an approved email/CRM endpoint before launch.

## 8. Section proportions

Long editorial sections alternate with denser scan surfaces. Reading text remains near 760px; site containers remain below 1320px. Dark navy is reserved for hero, process, formation feature and footer rhythm.

## 9. Content density

Useful depth beats short marketing fragments. Each service page answers relevance, assistance scope, likely documents, general process, case considerations, professional boundaries and next step.

## 10. Typography hierarchy

Make Fortune and Freemont demonstrate professional restraint through controlled scale and readable body copy. Hexa Beam uses a high-contrast editorial heading voice paired with neutral sans-serif UI and body text. Production may switch to licensed/self-hosted fonts after approval.

## 11. CTA patterns

Use contextual language such as “Discuss an inheritance case” or “Explore company formation.” Avoid “Book now” for sensitive corporate, tax or legal matters.

## 12. Colour usage

The site is predominantly white/warm-white, with navy institutional anchors and gold used for emphasis, hairlines and primary conversion actions. Gold gradients from the raster logo are not repeated as a decorative effect.

## 13. Card patterns

Borders and grid lines establish structure. Shadows appear only on interaction. Restrained square-corner service tiles replace pill-heavy or dashboard-style cards.

## 14. Sidebar-form strategy

Information-heavy pages use an approximately 70/30 desktop layout. The consultation card is sticky but returns to normal flow on smaller screens.

## 15. FAQ behaviour

Native accessible disclosure elements provide keyboard operation and a resilient no-JavaScript baseline.

## 16. Mobile adaptations

Mega navigation becomes a simple category list; sidebars return to flow; multi-column checklists, process steps and service grids collapse deliberately; CTAs become stacked; tap targets stay at least 44px.

## 17. Trust-building mechanisms

Process clarity, careful professional scope, explicit non-guarantees, regulatory citations, substantive FAQs and complete service information build trust without invented ratings, logos or metrics.

## 18. What Hexa Beam adopts

Reference-level depth, wide navigation, consultation availability, service-page completeness, connected-service cross-selling and strong footer findability.

## 19. What Hexa Beam improves

Calmer visual density, fewer promotional distractions, clearer distinction between direct and coordinated regulated work, a genuine service ecosystem beyond formation, and separate preventive/post-death estate propositions.

## 20. What Hexa Beam does not copy

No source code, wording, exact compositions, photography, pricing, awards, numerical claims, testimonials, logos, proprietary calculators or promotional package system is copied. The recurring diagonal “Beam” edge originates from the supplied HB monogram.
