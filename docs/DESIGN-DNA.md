# Hexa Beam design DNA

## Core idea

The identity is built around directional movement: a “beam” travels through complex UAE requirements and turns them into a visible route. Diagonal cuts, offset planes, fine route lines and asymmetrical crops derive from the HB monogram without redrawing the logo.

## Visual language

- Cinematic ink, midnight and slate gradients replace flat corporate navy.
- Champagne gold appears as a directional highlight, not a fill colour.
- Warm bone and ivory surfaces keep light sections architectural rather than clinical.
- Display typography carries the composition; small uppercase labels behave like editorial folios.
- Image stages use diagonal masks and layered line fields instead of rectangular stock-photo containers.
- Service discovery is a numbered editorial index rather than a grid of interchangeable cards.
- Dark immersive chapters alternate with minimal light fields to create rhythm and tension.

## Custom assets

- `beam-field.svg`: scalable diagonal line field, intersections and structural planes.
- `route-line.svg`: connected process route used behind the service-ecosystem explanation.
- `grain.svg`: subtle monochrome surface texture used globally at low opacity.

## Motion grammar

- Headline mask reveal on entry.
- Slow image settlement and restrained hero parallax.
- Directional service-index entrances.
- Gentle vertical chapter entrances for service tiles, process rows and proof points.
- Slow background-position drift on the formation image stage.

All motion is disabled when the user requests reduced motion.
